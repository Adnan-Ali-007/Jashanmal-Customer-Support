## 🎬 Recording Demo

For async demos:
1. Record screen showing chatbot interactions
2. Add voiceover explaining each feature
3. Show 3-4 key scenarios
4. End with call-to-action
5. Keep video under 5 minutes

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with their pain point** - If they complain about return calls, demo returns first
2. **Use realistic scenarios** - Don't just show happy path, show edge cases too
3. **Emphasize speed** - "This took 30 seconds vs 30 minutes with phone support"
4. **Show mobile** - Most customers use mobile, demo on phone screen
5. **Be confident** - This is production-ready technology, not a prototype
